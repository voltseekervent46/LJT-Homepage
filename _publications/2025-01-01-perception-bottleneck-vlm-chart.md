I am the first author of this work, which studies the perception bottleneck of vision-language models (VLMs) for chart understanding. Co-authors are Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, and Junxian He. The GitHub code repository is Vision4Chart.
